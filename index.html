Welcome

Aim at developing computational and theoretical methods for materials discovery in energy, mass, information, and human health applications. Research areas include physical chemistry, thermodynamics, statisical mechanics, theoretical & computational chemistry, AI for science, polymer science, interface engineering, biophysics & biomaterials, and nanotechnology. Research products include multi-scale simulation models for materials and biological systems, machine learning/artificial intelligence models for autonomous discovery, and theoretical models to uncover physics in materials. High Performance Computers (HPCs) and computational packages are used to solve research problems in materials discovery. Through rigorous calculation and interdisciplinary collaboration, our research will have broad impacts in Thermal Physics, Computational Chemistry, Physical Chemistry, Materials Science, and Data Science.

Research Areas of Interest

Thermodynamics

Understand energy, temperature, entropy, and phase/state transition from molecular level to macroscopic scale. Develop theoretical methods to solve thermal physics problems. link

Multiscale Simulation

Develop multi-scale computational methods, including ab initio, classical molecular dynamics (MD), coarse-grain (CG), Monte Carlo (MC), and hydrodynamic models. link

AI for Science

Integrate low-fidelity and high-quantity simulation dataset with high-fidelity and low-quantity experimental dataset to train ML/AI models, then apply physical rules to improve the accuracy. link
